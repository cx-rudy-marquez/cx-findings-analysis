Step one

Which projects would benefit most?

Each project's baseline is its most recent completed scan that actually ran the SAST engine — not simply its most recent scan. A run labelled “Full Scan” may have executed SCA only and carry no SAST results at all.

Two kinds of project are left out, so the counts below describe what is still worth doing: originals a re-onboarding already converted (named _FA_BACKUP), and projects that already scan with Findings Analysis enabled. Neither has a before-and-after left to measure.
